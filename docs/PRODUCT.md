# Product — AeroChrono

## Vision
AeroChrono is a mobile-first historical aviation companion for flight simulation.

**Tagline:** Choose an era. Choose an aircraft. Relive aviation history.

## Primary user journeys

### 1. Pick an era
The user selects a year or period and explores airlines, fleets, airports, liveries, registrations, and routes valid at that time.

### 2. Surprise Me
The user chooses constraints such as era, duration, region, airline, aircraft, and "only aircraft in my hangar". AeroChrono returns a historically compatible flight.

### 3. What was flying here?
The user selects an airport and date. AeroChrono shows known airlines, aircraft, destinations, and operations for that period.

### 4. Who flew this?
The user selects an aircraft type and date. AeroChrono shows operators, registrations, and known routes compatible with that time.

### 5. Historical tours
Users complete curated historical tours such as classic airline eras, retired airports, farewell operations, and aircraft-focused journeys.

### 6. Historical logbook
Users record completed flights and see statistics by decade, airline, aircraft, airport, country, and tour.

## Product principle
The historical dataset must be inspectable. Users should be able to understand why a route or fact is shown and how confident AeroChrono is in it.
